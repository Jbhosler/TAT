Based on the portfolio analysis, the current allocation is heavily concentrated in U.S. growth-oriented equities, with particularly large exposures to technology, artificial intelligence, and semiconductor-related investments. Technology alone represents approximately 46% of the equity allocation, while thematic exposures tied to artificial intelligence and semiconductors account for a significant portion of the portfolio's overall risk profile. The portfolio also exhibits a strong growth bias and relatively limited diversification across investment styles, sectors, and global markets.

Our recommendation is to transition the portfolio to the Auour Global Equity Strategy. While the strategy remains focused on long-term capital appreciation, it seeks to achieve that objective through broader diversification across regions, sectors, and investment factors. Rather than relying heavily on a narrow group of companies and themes to drive returns, the Global Equity Strategy is designed to participate in opportunities across a wider range of global markets and economic environments. This diversification can help reduce the portfolio's dependence on any single investment trend or market outcome.

The recommendation should not be viewed as a reduction in confidence regarding technology or artificial intelligence. Instead, it reflects a desire to manage risk prudently after a period in which a relatively small group of companies has generated exceptional returns and now trades at elevated valuations.

The current portfolio's concentration in these areas increases the potential impact of any future disappointment in earnings growth, competitive positioning, or investor sentiment. By broadening exposures and reducing concentration risk, we believe the Global Equity Strategy offers a more balanced approach to pursuing long-term growth while improving the portfolio's resilience across a wider range of market conditions.

Things to Note

Point-in-Time Estimate

Figures and calculations reflect a snapshot as of the date of this analysis. Anticipated gain realization is an estimate and subject to fluctuation based on market movement, execution timing, and pricing changes prior to trade date.

Risk Priority Over Tax Efficiency

A shift in market risk per our risk models may trigger a rebalance independent of this transition schedule, likely generating an additional taxable event. Principal protection supersedes tax minimization in all cases.

Prior Transactions Excluded

YTD transactions executed prior to this transition are not reflected in these calculations. Actual client tax liability may exceed the estimate shown.

Share Class Illustrative Only

Share classes displayed (e.g., A, C) are for illustrative purposes and do not reflect end-state holdings. Non-institutional share classes must be converted to institutional class prior to transition for advisory eligibility. Conversion is a non-taxable event.

Legacy Mutual Fund Distributions

Where legacy mutual funds are held in place of preferred ETFs, clients may incur additional taxable distributions (e.g., capital gains, dividend, or income distributions) generated by the fund itself. These distributions are outside our control, are not reflected in this transition's estimated tax impact, and may result in tax liability beyond what is shown here.
